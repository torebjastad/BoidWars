Pack Leader

You lead the blue flock. Grow by eating food and by devouring smaller flocks. Be the last flock standing, or the largest when the final countdown runs out.

A flock needs 1.2× your size (and 3 more boids) to eat you, and the same goes for you. Green rings are prey, red rings are danger.

Stragglers (a boid with no flockmate nearby) can be stolen by any flock, whatever its size. Big flocks are slower and slowly shed boids, and the arena shrinks over time.
